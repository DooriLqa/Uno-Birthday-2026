# Причал — рисованная сцена с нуля

Встроенный ImageGen: новая генерация только по тексту, БЕЗ передачи предыдущих изображений, БЕЗ локальных исправлений результата. Игровой референс бухты был осмотрен при подготовке описания.

Слева возвращены песчаная бухта, библиотека и лежаки на песке. Береговой помост отсутствует; короткий причал остаётся под камерой. Сохранены две лодки сбоку, корги-тотемы, знак карты, моряк под навесом и проход к правому пляжу. Направление стиля — матовая рисованная 2D-сцена с фактурой кисти.

Игровые файлы и код не заменены.

## Полный промпт

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Create a completely NEW image from a blank canvas. A finished HAND-PAINTED 2D background for a Hawaiian point-and-click adventure. One 8:3 panoramic landscape. This is a fresh painted composition, NOT a 3D render, NOT a patch of a previous image. The visual language is traditional animated adventure background painting: matte opaque gouache / brushed digital paint, deliberate flat color masses, softly painted form shadows, restrained edge highlights, warm expressive drawing, large irregular brush-shaped foliage groups, subtle wood grain. Vivid tropical color with natural tonal variation. NO glossy/plastic surfaces, no clay/Pixar/toy aesthetic, no raytraced shine, no waxy round stones, no photorealistic microdetail, no HDR rim-light on every leaf. Detailed enough for a game but painted, not airbrushed or muddy.
Story and composition:
Camera at human eye level near the SHORE END of a modest wooden pier, looking diagonally toward an inhabited Hawaiian beach landing. Just a short strip of sensible weathered planks enters below the viewer at bottom-center/right, roughly 12% image height; boards connect directly to dry ground, not a giant pier runway. Natural asymmetric shore, with water predominantly left, scenery rising behind, a sheltered sailor veranda at right-middle, and a wide visible exit to the beach at right. Do not build a town plaza.
CRITICAL LEFT SIDE: a genuine QUIET SANDY BEACH COVE, not a marina, not a wooden platform, not a cliff with buildings on it. Broad pale golden sand and a curving foam waterline fill the left middle ground. A continuous walkable sandy shore leads from central landing to this beach. On the sand near its palms stands a modest warm-plank LIBRARY HUT, steep straw-thatched gable roof, turquoise door and window frames, an open-book pictogram above its doorway, only a small ordinary porch attached to the hut. Two cream lounge chairs rest DIRECTLY ON SAND beneath one simple cream fabric shade canopy. Sand must be clearly visible around and in front of them. No boardwalk, no rope-fenced deck along this beach, no elevated platform replacing the cove. Library is a smaller left background destination, not center.
Small boats: two modest weathered wooden boats float quietly in water at the extreme left side near the main arrival pier, off the main sightline, separated hulls and sensible mooring. They must not occupy or hide the library beach. No elaborate dock complex; no platforms on the left beach.
CENTRAL LANDING: irregular natural dark basalt outcrops soften into packed sand with a few flat stepping stones, NOT a uniform cobbled plaza or straight seawall. Curve the shoreline gently in and out with rock clusters and small sandy pockets. A clear dirt-and-stone trail rises inland between TWO hand-carved wooden CORGI TOTEMS. Corgi carvings have upright ears, short broad foxlike muzzles, matte chisel-cut wooden heads and recessed stylized eyes, not furry animal heads. Carved paw and wave relief on posts, restrained turquoise and ochre pigment; readable crafted silhouettes, not giant monuments.
One yellow warning board beside the left totem: black folded-map silhouette with a yellow exclamation mark EXACTLY IN ITS CENTER. Black text on yellow in exactly two lines: "take a map!" and "возьми карту!". Legible lowercase words, no other text.
RIGHT-MIDDLE: modest weathered teal timber boathouse veranda, clear simple posts, simple roof construction, a SMALL warm shaded porch. An elderly anthropomorphic dog sailor, navy cap, blue-white striped shirt, light trousers, sits at a card table FULLY UNDER the roof. He is drawn in the same matte hand-painted 2D style as the environment, expressive but not a shiny 3D character; coherent paws/hands holding cards, two legs, one chair plus a spare chair. A lei and one woven lamp, sparse props.
RIGHT EDGE: a BROAD OPEN DRY SANDY PASSAGE curves around the veranda toward the tourist beach; at least 15% image width with its whole walkable surface visible. Do not block it with roof posts, shrubs or boulders. A distant single beach-supply hut with a broad thatched roof, turquoise posts, a colorful swim ring and small dark blue arcade cabinet establishes the next destination, a couple of beach parasols beyond.
Environment: recognizably Hawaiian fluted emerald mountain ridges, black volcanic lava, turquoise Pacific bay, coconut palms and hala, small hibiscus/plumeria clusters. Three small bamboo torches near inland route/veranda, flames separated from foliage. Warm diffuse tropical daylight and calm atmosphere. The land is connected throughout, all three walking exits are readable.
Keep a hand-painted 2D aesthetic across water, character, architecture, carving, plants and terrain. Simplify details into brushwork instead of rendering every leaf/rock pore. No added characters, signs beyond requested, UI, border, collage or watermark. Full opaque painted scene, not a transparent sprite.
```
